# Eval set

Five writing tasks that cover the formats the skill is most used for. Run them twice: once with SKILL.md loaded ("use the writing-style skill for all five tasks") and once without, then mark both sets against the checklist at the bottom.

## Tasks

1. **Slack message.** Announce to #general that an internal AI tool called "Scout" (auto-summarises customer support tickets) is now live for everyone. Mention a 30-min training session Thursday. Under 120 words.

2. **Email.** Ask an external partner (Sarah at a games studio) to reschedule the quarterly business review from next Tuesday to the following week because two team members will be travelling. Professional but warm. Under 150 words.

3. **LinkedIn post.** Share three lessons learned from deploying AI agents into production workflows at a blockchain gaming company over the past year. 150–200 words.

4. **One-pager intro.** The opening two paragraphs of an internal proposal recommending an automated lead-enrichment pipeline. Audience is the exec team. 100–150 words.

5. **Rewrite.** Make this paragraph sound natural and human:
   > "In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, businesses must leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to stay ahead of the curve. Our comprehensive platform empowers teams to seamlessly streamline their workflows, fostering a culture of innovation and unlocking unprecedented levels of productivity. It's not just a tool — it's a paradigm shift in how organisations navigate the complexities of digital transformation."

## Marking checklist

Score each piece pass/fail per row. A piece passes overall only if every row passes.

| Check | Fail examples |
|---|---|
| No banned words or phrases | leverage, seamless, delve, "excited to announce" |
| No em dashes | — anywhere outside quoted text |
| No Oxford comma | "a, b, and c" |
| Australian spelling | -ize, -or for -our, -er for -re, -yze |
| At most one triad per piece | two or more "x, y and z" lists |
| No "not X, but Y" template or variants | "it's not just a tool, it's a shift" |
| No rhetorical question + snappy answer | "The result? More sales." |
| No AI email furniture | "I hope this finds you well", "feel free to reach out" |
| No engagement bait or hashtag blocks | "What do you think? 👇 #AI" |
| No decorative emoji, max one exclamation mark | 🚀🎉 anywhere, "!" twice |
| Contractions in casual contexts | "it is" where "it's" reads naturally |
| Varied sentence rhythm | three consecutive sentences with identical shape |
